# UT99 Space Atmosphere Notes

The reusable atmosphere starts with the blue shine skybox because it gives the arena stronger silhouettes than the flatter black variants. It should feel close to late-90s arena shooters: hard color separation, cool ambient fill, dense blue distance fog, and restrained reflections instead of modern physically bright GI.

## Assets Created

- `Assets/Settings/Environment/UT99SpaceAtmosphere.asset`
- `Assets/Prefabs/Environment/PF_UT99SpaceAtmosphere.prefab`
- `Assets/Materials/Skyboxes/M_SpaceSkybox_UT99.mat` is generated by `GAUTNLET > Environment > Build UT99 Space Atmosphere Assets`.

Drop the prefab into any boot/menu scene to apply the look and keep it active across scene loads. The profile carries the six skybox faces directly, so it still works before the optional material asset is generated. Use `GAUTNLET > Environment > Apply UT99 Space Atmosphere To Open Scene` when you want the current scene's RenderSettings and bake settings written directly into the scene.

## Atmosphere Additions

- Add two or three huge, unlit, slow-rotating asteroid silhouettes outside the playable arena bounds.
- Add distant low-poly space station ribs or gothic antenna spires behind the map, with black materials and small cyan/magenta emissive strips.
- Add vertical energy beams or teleport-column meshes that extend past the arena roofline and pick up the blue fog.
- Add a few moving star glints as tiny additive billboard particles, kept sparse so the skybox does not turn noisy.
- Add colored team-side rim lights: cyan on one side, rusty amber or magenta on the other, very low intensity but high contrast.
- Add a thin horizon haze ring using an unlit transparent cylinder or torus around the map perimeter.
- Add occasional far lightning flashes by briefly boosting one directional or point light rather than brightening the whole world.
- For maps with lava, keep the sky cool and let lava supply the warm bounce so the scene gets that UT99 blue-vs-orange punch.
